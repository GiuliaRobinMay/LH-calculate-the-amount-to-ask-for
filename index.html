Your business stays your business

This tool was built the Lesko Help way: private, simple, and on your side.

Everything stays on your device

Your costs, your answers and your number are saved only in this browser, on this device — like notes on your own refrigerator. Nothing is sent to us, to Lesko Help, or to anyone else. There is no account, no email, no tracking of what you type.

Never type these anywhere in this tool

Your Social Security number, bank or card numbers, or passwords. This tool never asks for them — and neither does any honest program at the "how much do you need" stage. Anyone who demands your SSN plus a fee before they'll "release your grant" is a scammer.

Good to know
  • Clearing your browser data, or switching devices or browsers, means your saved number won't follow you — print it or copy it somewhere safe if you want a backup.
  • The "typical amounts" shown with your number are public figures from the programs themselves, with the date we last checked them. They are a guide, not a promise — what you get is decided by the people you ask.
  • This tool does one thing: it turns your costs into one clear amount with the reasons behind it. The community's guides, classes and Questions Channel take it from there.
